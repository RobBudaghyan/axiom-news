# iPhone check (Step 16, Part 3)

Short text for the owner to forward to a friend with an iPhone:

---

Hi! Could you help me test my news bot on your iPhone? It takes 5 minutes. 🙏

1. Update Telegram from the App Store first.
2. Open @justatia_bot in Telegram and tap Start.
3. Tap **Set up**. Go through the steps: language, topics, times, done. 📸 Screenshot each step.
4. Wait a minute: a **sample digest** comes in the chat. 📸 Screenshot it (scroll so the whole message shows).
5. Open **Settings** (the button next to the message box). 📸 Screenshot the top and the bottom.
6. Switch your iPhone to **dark mode** (Control Centre or Settings > Display). Open Settings again. 📸 Screenshot.
7. In Settings, under **My topics**, type a topic (for example Formula 1) and tap Add. 📸 Screenshot.
8. Tap **Help**. 📸 Screenshot. Go back.
9. Tap **Send feedback**, write "test from iPhone", tap Send. 📸 Screenshot.
10. Send me all the screenshots, and tell me if anything looked strange, cut off or slow.

Thank you! ❤️

---

What to look for in the screenshots (for me):
- The digest is one rich message (topic boxes, quote boxes, links), not an "update the app" placeholder.
- Settings: grey page with white cards (dark: black page, dark cards), the red switches, nothing
  cut off at the sides, the top bar the same colour as the page.
- Help: emoji titles, short lines, back arrow works.
- The new custom topic slides in with its own emoji.
- The friend appears in Admin on staging (the TEST bot is staging now). After the check, the friend's
  account can stay; it is a staging user only.
